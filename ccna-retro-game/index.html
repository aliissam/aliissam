HOW TO PLAY

ARCADE MODE (testing)

  • Clear 6 WORLDS, one per CCNA exam domain.
  • Each world is a run of questions. Answer with 1-4 or click/tap.
  • You have 20 seconds per question. The clock is part of the fight.
  • Correct = points + combo streak. Wrong or timeout = lose a ♥ life.
  • Every world ends in a BOSS QUESTION worth double points.
  • Lose all 3 lives and it's GAME OVER — but your progress on cleared worlds is saved.
  • Clear all 6 worlds to become a CCNA CHAMPION.
  • After every answer you'll see a short explanation of the correct answer.

STUDY MODE (learning)

  • No timer, no lives, no score — just you and the material.
  • Pick any world (all unlocked) and flip through it question by question.
  • Hit SHOW ANSWER to reveal the correct choice and its explanation.
  • Use PREV/NEXT to move at your own pace, in any order.
